# VELOOP Rewards Level-Up Dashboard & Gaming Arcade

A state-of-the-art, production-quality **Fintech Rewards, Level-Up Ecosystem & Gaming Arcade** built with **React 19**, **Vite**, **Bootstrap Grid**, and **CSS Modules**. Designed with a deep navy palette (`#161827`), refined gold and silver reward accents, restrained animations, and a full suite of skill-based arcade games with a **Points-to-VE Coins & Level XP Conversion Engine**.

## 🕹️ The 5 Playable Arcade Games

All games run at 60 FPS, award arcade points directly into the **Points Vault**, and link directly to the **Points Converter**:

### 1. 🏃 Cyber Surfers: Neon Run (Subway Surfers-Style 3D Runner)
- **Engine**: Pseudo-3D canvas perspective with vanishing horizon, 3 lanes, and high-speed neon railway ties.
- **Controls**:
  - `←` / `→` or `A` / `D` (or touch left/right buttons / swipe) to switch tracks
  - `↑` or `W` / `Space` to **Jump** over low energy hurdles and collect airborne coins
  - `↓` or `S` to **Slide** under high red laser gates
- **Collectibles**: VE Gold coins, 🧲 Coin Magnet (8-second auto-pull), and distance points.

### 2. 🍬 Crypto Crush: Token Match-3 (Candy Crush-Style Puzzle)
- **Engine**: 7x7 interactive token grid with smooth cascading gravity and explosive chain combos.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Swap adjacent tokens (Gold Coins, Sapphire Gems, Ruby Crystals, Amethyst Shards, Emerald Nodes) to create rows or columns of 3, 4, or 5.
  - Multiplier cascade streaks (+1.5x, +2.0x points).
  - 15-move tactical limit with a target goal of 600 points.

### 3. 🔗 Fintech Connections (NYT Connections-Style Association Game)
- **Engine**: 4x4 matrix of 16 financial and crypto terms.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Find 4 hidden groups of 4 words that share a secret connection (e.g. *VELOOP Member Tiers*, *Digital Assets*, *Security Protocols*, *Fintech Yield Accelerators*).
  - 4 mistakes allowed before full reveal.
  - Color-coded category ribbons (Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple) and deduction feedback.

### 4. 🧠 Neuro Matrix: Brain Test (Cognitive Pattern & Memory Matrix)
- **Engine**: 4x4 cybernetic node grid testing spatial sequence recall.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Watch glowing nodes flash sequentially with synthesized audio tones.
  - Repeat the exact pattern. Each stage adds more nodes to test and expand working memory.
  - Awards up to +1,000 points and calculates your Cognitive IQ Rating.

### 5. 🪙 VE Coin Catch (Asset Interception Mini-Game)
- **Engine**: 30-second skill-based asset catcher with magnetic collector dock.
- **Mechanics**: Catch gold coins (+10 pts), sapphire orbs (+25 pts), and diamond stars (+50 pts) while dodging hazard glitch nodes (-15 pts).

## 🪙 Points-to-Coins & XP Conversion Engine

- **Treasury Pool**: All game points earned across the 5 arcade games flow into your **Unconverted Points Balance**.
- **Exchange Machine**:
  - **Rate**: Every **100 points** = **+10 VE Coins** (spendable wallet cash) + **+25 Level XP** (advancing your tier!).
  - **Presets & Slider**: Convert 100, 250, 500, or All points with live output previews.
  - **Instant Deposit**: Credits your **VE Coins Wallet**, injects **Level XP**, triggers the **Level-Up Celebration Modal** when thresholds are crossed, and appends a verified record to the **Activity Ledger**.
- **VE Coins Rewards Shop**:
  - 💳 **$10.00 Digital Reward Card** (500 Coins)
  - ⚡ **24-Hour 1.5x Multiplier Boost** (350 Coins)
  - 🛡️ **Zero-Fee Priority Settlement Pass** (200 Coins)
  - 👑 **Founder Gold Insignia Crest** (800 Coins)

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework**: [React 19](https://react.dev/) + [Vite 8](https://vite.dev/)
- **Routing**: [React Router 7](https://reactrouter.com/) (Configured at `/Lvl-Dashboard`)
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS Modules (`*.module.css`) + Bootstrap 5 Grid/utilities
- **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Audio**: Custom Synthesizer using the browser **Web Audio API**
- **State Management**: React Context (`RewardsContext`) with `localStorage` persistence
